PRIVACY POLICY

Privacy without the fine print.

Effective 9 October 2026

Summary

CreatorBucket is built to organize footage on your Android device. We do not collect, sell, share, or transmit your footage, project names, notes, or usage data to our servers.

Data stored on your device

CreatorBucket stores bucket names, notes, clip records, and imported project copies on your device. Project copies are stored in CreatorBucket’s app-specific storage so they are not indexed as duplicate videos by Gallery apps.

Footage access

You choose which videos to share with CreatorBucket through Android’s Share sheet or file picker. The app reads selected footage only to copy it into the chosen project. If you select Move after copy, Android shows its own confirmation before the original local media is deleted.

Sharing and exports

When you choose to share clips with an editor or copy a project to a folder or SSD, Android sends those files to the app or location you select. CreatorBucket does not control how those destinations handle your data.

No accounts, advertising, or tracking

CreatorBucket does not require an account. It has no ads, analytics SDKs, crash reporting SDKs, or third-party tracking.

Retention and deletion

You can delete clips or complete buckets from within the app. Uninstalling CreatorBucket removes its app-private project copies and local app data. Export any active project before uninstalling.

Changes and contact

We may update this policy when the app changes. Questions about this policy can be sent through the CreatorBucket support page.
