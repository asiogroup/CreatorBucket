Using CreatorBucket

CreatorBucket helps you organize video files that you select on your Android device. You are responsible for the footage you import, share, export, and delete.

Your footage

You retain ownership of your footage. CreatorBucket does not upload or claim ownership of it.

Back up important work

CreatorBucket stores working project copies locally. Export projects you need to retain. Deleting clips, finishing a project, clearing app data, or uninstalling the app can remove local project copies.

Move after copy

Move after copy asks Android to remove selected local camera originals after CreatorBucket has made a project copy. Review Android’s confirmation carefully. Cloud services may keep their own copies and can restrict deletion.

The app is provided as available. Features may change as CreatorBucket develops.
