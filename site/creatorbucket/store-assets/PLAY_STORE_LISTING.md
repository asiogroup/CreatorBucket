## Short description (80 characters or fewer)

Organize camera footage into projects. Share, preview, and export each shoot.

## Full description

CreatorBucket keeps your camera footage organised by project without changing the way you shoot.

Record with your normal Android camera. When you are ready to sort the footage, select videos in Gallery or Files, tap Share, and choose CreatorBucket. Add the clips to a named bucket for that project.

Inside each project, preview footage, add a shot list or note, see the total filming time, and remove clips added by mistake. When the project is ready, share its clips to an editing app or copy the whole folder to an SSD or another selected location.

FEATURES

• Import one or multiple camera videos through Android’s Share sheet
• Create named project buckets for each shoot
• Choose Copy originals or Move after copy
• Keep CreatorBucket’s project copies out of Gallery
• Preview clips before editing
• Add notes, edit ideas, and shot lists to a project
• Track clip count, total duration, and storage size
• Delete individual clips or complete an entire project
• Share clips directly with compatible editing apps
• Copy a complete project to an SSD or a selected folder
• Choose Footage timeline, Dashboard tiles, or Project gallery for the home view
• Clean, Creative, and Dark themes

YOUR FOOTAGE STAYS UNDER YOUR CONTROL

CreatorBucket does not require an account and does not upload footage to a server. Footage and project information stay on your device. Copy is the default import mode. When you choose Move after copy, Android always asks for confirmation before it removes a local camera original.

IMPORTANT

CreatorBucket’s working copies are stored in app-private storage. Export projects you want to retain before uninstalling the app.
