CreatorBucket support

Help for organizing your footage.

Getting started

  1. Record videos with your usual Android camera.
  2. In Gallery or Files, select clips and tap Share.
  3. Choose CreatorBucket, then select or create a project bucket.
  4. Open the bucket to preview, add notes, export, or share its clips.

Why are my originals still in Gallery?

Copy is the default. For Move after copy, select the setting in CreatorBucket, share local camera videos from Gallery or Files, and approve Android’s deletion prompt. Cloud apps such as Google Photos or Drive may not let CreatorBucket delete their originals.

Why are exported clips named differently?

CreatorBucket preserves existing files in the destination. It adds a number when a filename already exists instead of overwriting footage.

How do I remove a project?

Swipe left on a project or open it and select Done with this project. Confirming deletes CreatorBucket’s project copies. It does not change your original camera clips.

Contact

For support, include your Android version, CreatorBucket version, and a short description of the issue. A support email address will be added before the public Google Play release.
