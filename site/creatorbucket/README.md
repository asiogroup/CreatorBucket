# CreatorBucket website

Upload the **contents** of this directory to `https://techtippr.com/creatorbucket/`.

Pages are ready at these paths:

- `/creatorbucket/`
- `/creatorbucket/privacy/`
- `/creatorbucket/terms/`
- `/creatorbucket/support/`
- `/creatorbucket/install/`

Before publishing the Google Play listing, add a real support email address to `support/index.html`. The Play Console listing and privacy policy should use the same contact address.

When the app is released, replace the test-build link on `index.html` and the Google Play text on `install/index.html` with its official Play Store URL.
