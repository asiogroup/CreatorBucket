A SIMPLE WORKFLOW

Film normally. Sort when you share.

01

Select and share

Choose one or more videos in Gallery or Files, then tap Share and select CreatorBucket.

02

Pick a project

Create a bucket or add the footage to one you already have. Use copy or move-after-copy.

03

Review the shoot

Preview clips, add a shot list, delete mistakes, and see each project’s total duration.

04

Export when ready

Send footage to an editing app or copy the whole project to an SSD or folder.

MADE FOR REAL FOOTAGE

Your camera roll stays usable.

CreatorBucket stores its working copies in private app storage, so imported clips do not turn up as duplicate videos in Gallery. Originals stay where they are unless you choose Move after copy and approve Android’s deletion request.

  • Named project buckets
  • Clip preview and multi-select deletion
  • Notes for shot lists and edit ideas
  • Footage duration, clip count, and storage totals
  • Clean, Creative, and Dark themes
